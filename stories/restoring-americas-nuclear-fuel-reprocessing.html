Restoring America's Nuclear Fuel Reprocessing Life Cycle

October 2026 · Keith J Maxwell

A policy white paper on moving U.S. spent nuclear fuel from the liability to the asset column by restoring a closed fuel cycle, lowering costs for existing and new reactor builds while reducing long-term environmental risk, federal liabilities, and proliferation concerns. It covers the financial case, history, international comparison, and the statutory, regulatory, and treaty changes required, and how to modernize U.S. nuclear export controls.
