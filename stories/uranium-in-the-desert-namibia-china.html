Uranium in the Desert: From a Contested Colonial Resource to Major Global Supplier for China

October 2026 · Keith J Maxwell

Traces Namibia's uranium industry from the 1928 discovery near the Rössing Mountains and the controversial colonial-era Rössing mine to its rise as a top-three global producer, explaining why China, now the dominant investor in Namibia's major mines, is so interested in the country, which has a strong nuclear regulator but no nuclear power plants.
