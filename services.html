Services

Maxwell Associates offers consultation in the following areas.

Project Management

Scope, schedule and stakeholder coordination for complex technical projects, drawing on decades of project management for nuclear utilities, OEMs and contract engineering firms, including power uprate, sump, spent fuel and decontamination projects.

Engineering

Systems and mechanical engineering, including design calculations, equipment specifications, design and licensing basis reviews, operability determinations and independent technical assessments.

Construction

Owner oversight of modifications and new builds: RFQs, specifications, proposal evaluation, vendor selection, drawing review and approval, and field installation support.

Real Estate

Guidance on property evaluation, development planning, due diligence coordination, and ongoing asset and project management.

Telecommunications

Support for telecom infrastructure planning, site and deployment coordination, vendor management, and project delivery.

Naval Architecture

Consulting on vessel design and modification, including technical review of arrangements, structures and systems, feasibility assessment and specification development.

Merchant Shipyard

Consulting for merchant shipyard projects: planning and scheduling of new construction, repair and overhaul work, specification and vendor coordination, and oversight through sea trials and delivery.
