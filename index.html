About

Keith J Maxwell is a senior systems and project engineer with more than 30 years of experience serving BWR and PWR nuclear utilities, nuclear OEMs, and U.S. government facilities. His work spans extended power uprates, design and licensing basis recovery, safety-related modifications, and project management at stations across the country. A Massachusetts Maritime Academy marine engineering graduate and former U.S. Naval Reserve officer (1981–1993), he also holds an active U.S. Coast Guard Third Assistant Engineer license and has merchant marine operating experience, including contract work with Military Sealift Command and service as Assistant Engineer / Oiler with the Massachusetts Steamship Authority. He brings that background to Maxwell Associates.
